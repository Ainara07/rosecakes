Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I place my order?

We recommend placing your order at least 3 days before your celebration.

Can I request a custom cake design?

Yes. You can describe your preferred colors, decorations and design in the Special Requests field.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. You can choose between pickup and delivery when completing the order form.

Can I add a message to the cake?

Yes. Enter the message you would like on your cake in the Cake Message field.
